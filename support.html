Breach — Support

Breach: Murder Mysteries · Developer: Bader Kamal

Questions, a puzzle that seems wrong, or a purchase that didn't unlock? Email baderkamal@gmail.com and include your device model and the case number (for example "Vol. 2 · Case 14"). You'll get a reply within a few days.

Restoring purchases

Unlocked volumes are tied to your store account, not to the device. On a new phone or after reinstalling, open Settings → Restore purchases (or the unlock screen → Restore purchases) while signed in to the same account you bought with.

Progress

Your progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress; it cannot be recovered from a server because none is kept.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by the store you bought from. On iPhone and iPad, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Language

Breach follows your device language (English, Arabic, French, Italian, Spanish, Turkish, Russian, Simplified or Traditional Chinese). To change it, open Settings → Language.
