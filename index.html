Breach — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026 · Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Developer: Bader Kamal · Contact: baderkamal@gmail.com

Breach is a logic-puzzle app. It is designed to work without collecting personal information. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not do with data.

What the app stores on your device

Your puzzle progress (grid marks, answers, solved/revealed status), your language choice, and a cached list of the volumes you have unlocked. This data stays on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. To restore your unlocks across devices and installs, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous, randomly generated app-user identifier, the app's vendor device identifier on iPhone and iPad (IDFV, which is unique to this developer's apps and cannot be used to track you across other companies' apps; used only to match and restore purchases), and your purchase records (product identifiers, purchase dates, store receipts). RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address or any puzzle data. The developer can see aggregate sales figures and anonymous purchase records only.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-up, no email or name collection.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Links to other websites

The app links to Amazon for the printed editions of the books and to this policy. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game with fictional murder-mystery themes (no graphic content). It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Delete the app to remove all locally stored progress. To request deletion of anonymous purchase records held by RevenueCat, email the address above with the store receipt or approximate purchase date.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
